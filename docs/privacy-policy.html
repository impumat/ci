PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: July 28, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how IMPUMAT collects, uses, and protects information that may be collected when using the website www.impumat.com, its Progressive Web App (PWA), and the IMPUMAT Accounting Software Android application distributed through Google Play.

IMPUMAT is committed to protecting the privacy of its users and to applying the principles of transparency and security in the processing of personal information.

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the IMPUMAT website, its Progressive Web App (PWA), and its Android application distributed through Google Play as a Trusted Web Activity (TWA).

3. Information Collected

The IMPUMAT application does not directly collect personal information required for its operation.

However, certain information may be voluntarily provided by users when they:

  • Contact IMPUMAT by email;
  • Use a contact form, where available;
  • Make a purchase through a partner platform.

The information collected is limited to what users choose to provide.

4. Payments

Payments for IMPUMAT products are processed exclusively through Payhip, an online marketplace specializing in the distribution of digital products.

Transactions are handled directly by Payhip according to its own security procedures.

IMPUMAT does not collect, process, or store users' banking or payment information.

5. Use of Information

Any information voluntarily provided is used only to:

  • Respond to users' requests;
  • Provide order follow-up and customer support;
  • Improve the services offered;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

No personal information is sold or transferred for commercial purposes.

6. Cookies

The IMPUMAT website may use technical cookies only when they are necessary for the proper functioning of the website and to improve the user experience

At this time, IMPUMAT does not use cookies for advertising or marketing tracking purposes.

7. Hosting

The IMPUMAT website is hosted by a specialized provider. As part of normal hosting operations, certain technical data (such as IP address, date and time of access, or browser type) may be automatically recorded in technical logs to ensure the operation, security, and maintenance of the service.

IMPUMAT does not use this data for commercial purposes.

8. Third-Party Services

The IMPUMAT website and application may rely on third-party services for certain features, including:

  • FormSubmit, for the secure delivery of messages submitted through the contact form;
  • Payhip, for secure payment processing and digital product distribution;
  • Google Play, for the distribution of the Android application.

These third-party services maintain their own Privacy Policies and process data in accordance with their own Terms of Use. IMPUMAT does not control these external policies.

9. Data Security

IMPUMAT implements reasonable measures to protect the information provided against unauthorized access, use, modification, or disclosure.

10. Users' Rights

Users may request information regarding the personal data they have voluntarily provided and may request its correction or deletion, subject to applicable legal obligations.

11. Changes to the Privacy Policy

IMPUMAT reserves the right to modify this Privacy Policy to reflect legal, technical, or operational changes.

The date of the latest update appears at the top of this page.
